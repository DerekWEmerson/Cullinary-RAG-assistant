Ultimate Ingredient Substitutions Guide: Cooking Without the Missing Items

Primary Image
Written By: Catherine Boeckmann Executive Digital Editor and Master Gardener
November 20, 2025
Body

Out of eggs, milk, or that obscure ingredient your recipe calls for? Don’t panic—your kitchen is full of hidden solutions! From pantry staples to clever hacks, we’ve got substitutions that keep your culinary creations on track (and tasting delicious).

Even uncommon or exotic ingredients can be substituted with others in your pantry or with ones that are easier (and a lot less expensive) to get. For example, if you don’t have bamboo shoots, use asparagus. Need shallots? Red or Spanish onions do nicely. Out of tamarind juice? Combine 5 parts ketchup and 1 part vinegar.  

Check below for other simple subs—cook, eat, and enjoy!

→ Low on flour? Here are some baking ideas. 

Never get stuck mid-recipe again! Bookmark this ultimate ingredient substitution guide and keep it handy in your kitchen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I substitute baking powder if I only have baking soda?

Yes! Use 1/4 teaspoon baking soda plus 1/4 teaspoon cornstarch and 1/2 teaspoon cream of tartar to replace 1 teaspoon of baking powder.

What can I use instead of buttermilk in a recipe?

Combine 1 tablespoon lemon juice or vinegar with 1 cup milk and let it sit for 5 minutes. You can also use plain yogurt.

How do I replace eggs in baking?

Options include 3 tablespoons mayonnaise, 2 1/2 tablespoons powdered egg substitute plus water, or half a mashed banana with 1/2 teaspoon baking powder.

What can replace coconut milk in cooking?

Mix 2 1/2 cups of water with 2 cups of unsweetened shredded coconut, boil, cool, blend, and strain.

How can I swap exotic spices or herbs I don’t have?

Many herbs have similar flavor profiles. For example, bergamot can be replaced with mint, and lemongrass can be substituted with lemon zest.
